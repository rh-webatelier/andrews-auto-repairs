Who we are

This website is run by Andrew's Auto Repairs Co. Ltd (company number 05480067), Unit 1 Darwen House, Darwen Street, Manchester M16 9HT. Registered office: Charter House, 33 Greek Street, Stockport SK3 8AX. For any privacy question, email ands.p@me.com or call 0161 834 5035.

Booking and contact form

When you send a booking request we receive your name, phone number and, if you give them, your email address, vehicle registration, the service you need, a preferred day and your message. We use this only to reply to you and arrange the work. Our lawful basis is taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, and our legitimate interest in answering enquiries.

The form is delivered to our inbox by a third-party form service that processes the data on our behalf. We don’t use your details for marketing and we don’t sell or share them.

MOT checker

If you use the MOT checker, the registration you enter is sent to the DVSA MOT History API to look up the vehicle’s public MOT record. We don’t store the registration or the result.

Maps

The map on this site only loads if you choose to show it. Google Maps may then set cookies and collect data under Google’s own privacy policy.

Cookies

This website doesn’t set any tracking or advertising cookies. Our hosting provider (Cloudflare) may process technical data such as IP addresses to keep the site secure and working.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or object to how we use it. Get in touch using the details above. If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.
